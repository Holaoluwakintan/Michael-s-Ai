Caring for Mom or Dad shouldn’t fall on one person. 4 ways to share the load as a family:
1. Talk early and together, before things feel urgent
2. List what needs doing each week: meals, errands, housework, check-in calls
3. Split it by strengths: family nearby helps in person, family far away handles calls and planning
4. Keep your loved one at the center and ask what matters most to them
